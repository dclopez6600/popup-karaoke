Why Karaoke Works for School & Community Events

When you're planning a school carnival, fundraiser, or community event, you need entertainment that pulls people in, keeps them engaged, and works for every age group in the crowd — from kindergartners to grandparents. Karaoke checks every box. It's participatory, it's joyful, and it creates moments that people genuinely remember. A child getting up in front of their whole school to sing a song they love? That's confidence-building in action. A parent getting on the mic for the first time in decades? That's community. PopUp Karaoke brings all the equipment, an experienced host, and a song library that's fully filterable for age-appropriate content — so you can focus on running your event, not managing entertainment logistics.

We've powered PTA fundraisers, spring carnivals, church fall festivals, and nonprofit galas throughout Lake County and beyond. Every event comes with full professional setup, $1M liability insurance, and a host who knows how to work a multigenerational crowd.

How to Make It Family-Friendly

  • ✓ Enable explicit content filtering. Let us know your audience age range when you book — we'll have the library pre-filtered before we arrive so nothing inappropriate can be searched or queued.
  • ✓ Run themed song rounds. "Disney round," "90s hits," or "country classics" give kids and parents a category to rally around and make group singing more likely.
  • ✓ Set up a dedicated kids' queue. Our host can run a separate sign-up sheet for younger kids so they get on the mic without fighting the adult queue — builds confidence and keeps it fair.
  • ✓ Use karaoke as a paid activity. Many PTA events charge $2–5 per song or sell "jump the queue" passes as a fundraiser. This adds a revenue stream without requiring extra setup on your end.
  • ✓ Encourage group and family performances. Our host actively invites parent-child duets and family performances — these become the most memorable moments of the event and get the best crowd reaction.

School Karaoke FAQ

Is the song library appropriate for kids? +

Yes. We can filter out explicit content and curate a completely family-friendly song list. Our 50,000+ song library includes Disney hits, pop, country, classic rock, and more. Just let us know your audience age range when you book — we'll have the filter set before we arrive.

Do you carry liability insurance? +

Yes. PopUp Karaoke carries $1M general liability insurance. We can provide a certificate of insurance (COI) to your school district, church, or nonprofit upon request — just mention it when you book and we'll have it ready well before your event.

How many kids can participate? +

Our standard setup handles up to 300 attendees comfortably. An open-mic rotation keeps everyone involved — kids sign up, our host calls names, and the queue moves fast enough that nobody waits too long. For events over 300, reach out and we'll discuss extended options.

Can we use karaoke as a fundraising activity? +

Absolutely. Many of our school and nonprofit clients charge admission to the karaoke activity, sell "jump the queue" passes, or hold sing-off contests with entry fees. Karaoke naturally draws a crowd and keeps people on-site longer — which means more revenue across all your fundraising activities.

How far in advance should we book for a school event? +

At least 4 weeks in advance — and earlier if your event falls during spring carnival season (March through May), which is our busiest period. Summer church festivals and fall fundraisers also book quickly. Reach out as soon as your date is confirmed.

Do you need special permits for school events? +

Typically no — karaoke entertainment doesn't require a special permit. However, we're happy to provide our insurance certificate and any vendor documentation your school district, church, or organization requires. Just let us know what paperwork is needed when you reach out.
