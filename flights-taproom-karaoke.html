About the Flights Taproom Residency

Flights Taproom & Whiskey Lounge on 169th St is Hammond's go-to spot for a sophisticated night out — an extensive whiskey collection, craft beers on tap, and great vibes. Every 1st and 3rd Tuesday, PopUp Karaoke transforms it into Hammond's most entertaining night of the week.

We bring a full concert-quality setup: pro sound system, LED stage lighting, wireless microphones, and a 75,000+ song library. The intimate taproom setting means everyone can hear each other, cheer each other on, and feel like a star. Whether you're a whiskey-sipping regular or a first-time karaoke singer, Tuesday nights at Flights are welcoming, fun, and just a little bit wild.

The Flights crowd has personality. You'll find regulars who have their go-to songs memorized, newcomers being talked into the mic by their friends, and everyone in between. The host keeps the queue moving, the energy up, and the laughs coming all night.

What to Expect

  • Show up by 8pm — early birds get first spots in the queue
  • Explore the whiskey menu — Flights has one of the best selections in Hammond
  • Submit your song at the bar or through the live queue
  • Sing your heart out — the crowd here is warm, enthusiastic, and supportive
  • Stay for the late sets — the energy peaks around 9:30 PM
