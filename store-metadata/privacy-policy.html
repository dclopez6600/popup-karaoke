Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 18, 2026

PopUp Karaoke ("we", "us", or "our") operates the PopUp Karaoke mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App.

Information We Collect

The App does not require you to create an account to browse events, the song catalog, or reviews. We may collect anonymous usage analytics (e.g., which screens are most visited) to improve the App experience. We do not sell or share your data with third parties for advertising purposes.

Event & Song Data

Event listings, the song catalog, and reviews displayed in the App are pulled from our servers in real time. This data is read-only from the user's perspective and does not require any personal information.

Push Notifications

If you opt in to push notifications, your device token is stored securely to deliver event reminders and live event alerts. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

Third-Party Services

The App is built on Expo and React Native. Standard device information (OS version, device model) may be collected by these platforms for crash reporting and performance monitoring. Please review Expo's privacy policy at expo.dev/privacy.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page.
