PopUp Karaoke — Your Night. Your Stage.

The ultimate companion app for karaoke lovers and event-goers. PopUp Karaoke brings the excitement of live karaoke nights to your pocket, with real-time updates that keep you in the loop no matter where you are.

🎤 WHAT'S HAPPENING RIGHT NOW
See live karaoke events in your area, watch the singer rotation update in real time, and follow along with who's performing — all without refreshing.

📅 NEVER MISS AN EVENT
Browse the full event calendar, see venue details, capacity, and ratings at a glance. Filter by tonight's shows, live events, or the full week ahead.

🎵 EXPLORE THE SONG CATALOG
Search thousands of songs by title, artist, genre, decade, key, or BPM. Save favorites for quick reference when it's your turn.

⭐ REAL REVIEWS FROM REAL SINGERS
Read honest reviews of events and venues from the karaoke community. See ratings, crowd vibes, and host quality before you show up.

👥 COMMUNITY FEED
Follow the action with a live social feed — check-ins from friends, achievements earned, songs being sung right now, and more.
