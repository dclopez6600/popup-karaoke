Best Karaoke Venues in Northwest Indiana 2026

Northwest Indiana — Gary, Hammond, Hobart, Crown Point, Valparaiso — doesn't get enough credit for its nightlife. The Region has a genuine bar culture, great food scenes, and increasingly, a karaoke scene worth talking about. This is your 2026 guide to the best places to sing in NW Indiana.

Quick Look: The PopUp Karaoke Schedule in NW Indiana

18th Street Brewery · Gary Every Wednesday · 7–10 PM
El Capitán · Hobart Every Friday · 8 PM–12 AM
Flights Taproom · Hammond 1st & 3rd Tuesdays · 8–11 PM

All shows are free to attend. Full calendar at popupkaraoke.net/#events.

The Venues

01
18th Street Brewery — Gary, IN
5725 Miller Ave, Gary, IN · Every Wednesday, 7:00 PM – 10:00 PM
Every Wednesday Craft Beer Free to Attend Gary · Miller Beach

18th Street Brewery is one of the most decorated craft breweries in Indiana — multiple national and international awards, a rotating tap list of 20+ beers brewed on-site, and a laid-back taproom that consistently draws a great crowd. Their Miller Ave location in Gary is the home base for our flagship weekly karaoke night.

Wednesday nights at 18th Street are a rhythm you can set your week to. The crowd is a mix of craft beer regulars, karaoke veterans, and first-timers who got dragged in and never want to leave. The vibe is inclusive, energetic, and genuinely fun — not performative or competitive. People are there to sing and have a good time, and our host sets exactly that tone from the first song.

The brewery's award-winning beers are a major part of the appeal. You're not just singing — you're at one of Indiana's best taprooms. Coming in on a Wednesday and leaving by 7:30 is physically impossible once you realize how good the beer is.

  • Best for: Weeknight regulars, craft beer fans, groups of 4–12 who want a relaxed but lively night out
  • Get there by: 7pm to secure good seats and sign up for an early queue slot
  • Don't miss: The seasonal releases and brewery exclusives that only show up on the taproom menu
→ Full 18th Street Brewery karaoke page with upcoming dates
02
El Capitán Seafood & Mexican Grill — Hobart, IN
327 Main St, Hobart, IN · Every Friday, 8:00 PM – 12:00 AM
Every Friday Till Midnight Seafood + Mexican Free to Attend Hobart Downtown

El Capitán is Hobart's hidden gem — a seafood and Mexican fusion restaurant that draws from across the Region for its food alone. Add karaoke every Friday and you have one of the best nights out in NW Indiana, full stop.

The 4-hour block sets El Capitán apart. Most public karaoke nights wrap at 11pm. El Capitán's goes till midnight, which means the night has real room to breathe. The early hours are dinner-and-drinks energy; by 10pm, the room has shifted into something that feels more like a concert. The late-night crowd at El Capitán is a real thing.

The bilingual experience here is also worth noting. El Capitán draws a diverse crowd, and our Spanish-language catalog — reggaeton, cumbia, banda, boleros, and modern Latin pop — gets heavy rotation. Nights here often swing between English and Spanish throughout the queue, which creates an energy you won't find at many karaoke nights in the region.

  • Best for: Date nights, bilingual crowds, anyone who wants a full late-night karaoke experience
  • Pro tip: Eat dinner before singing — the food is genuinely excellent and El Capitán's kitchen stays busy
  • Don't miss: The late-night stretch around 10:30–11:30 when the room hits its peak
→ Full El Capitán karaoke page with upcoming dates
03
Flights Taproom — Hammond, IN
839 169th St, Hammond, IN · 1st & 3rd Tuesdays, 8:00 PM – 11:00 PM
1st & 3rd Tuesdays Craft Beer Flights Free to Attend Hammond

Flights Taproom is a Hammond craft beer bar built around the concept of beer exploration — their signature offering is samplers and flights of local and regional craft beers. The bar has a warm, social atmosphere that makes it a natural fit for karaoke, and the regulars here have become some of the most enthusiastic karaoke participants in the region.

The 1st and 3rd Tuesday schedule gives Flights a different character than the weekend shows. Tuesday night karaoke attracts people who are genuinely there for the experience — not just looking for something to do on a Friday. The queue tends to be tighter, the singing tends to be better, and the crowd interaction tends to be more engaged. If you want to get good at karaoke (or feel good about how good you already are), Tuesday at Flights is your night.

Hammond's location — right on the border with Chicago, easy access from the south suburbs — means Flights draws from a wide geographic area. It's one of the first NW Indiana stops for people coming in from the city, and it shows in the crowd diversity.

  • Best for: Karaoke regulars, craft beer enthusiasts, smaller groups who want a more intimate night
  • Get there by: 8pm — the queue fills fast on popular Tuesdays
  • Don't miss: The beer flight menu — this is genuinely a great beer bar with 20+ taps

What Makes NW Indiana Karaoke Different

People who've done karaoke in Chicago and then come out to a PopUp Karaoke show at one of our NW Indiana residencies often say the same thing: the crowd is more real here. There's less performance anxiety about performing in front of strangers — people are looser, more encouraging, and quicker to sing something unexpected.

Part of it is the venue culture. 18th Street, El Capitán, and Flights are all neighborhood spots with regulars who've built genuine community around them. When PopUp Karaoke shows up, we're entering an existing social fabric, not just filling a room with strangers. That's why the energy at these shows builds the way it does.

The other part is production quality. These aren't laptop-and-YouTube karaoke nights. Our setup — QSC/Yamaha PA, professional stage lighting, wireless mics, 75,000+ song library — creates a real show environment. When singers sound and look good, they perform with more confidence. When performers have confidence, the crowd responds. It compounds quickly.

Planning your first NW Indiana karaoke night? Wednesday at 18th Street Brewery is the easiest entry point — great beer, consistent weekly show, welcoming crowd. If you want a late-night experience, El Capitán every Friday is the move.

The Rest of NW Indiana: What Else Is Out There

Beyond our three residencies, NW Indiana has a scattered but real karaoke scene across the Region. Crown Point, Valparaiso, Merrillville, and Portage all have bars that host occasional or weekly karaoke nights. The quality varies significantly — sound systems, song libraries, and hosting all over the map. The most consistent professional karaoke experience in the region remains with PopUp Karaoke.

If you're a bar in Crown Point, Schererville, Munster, Lowell, or elsewhere in the Region looking to add a karaoke night, get in touch. We're actively looking to expand our NW Indiana footprint.
