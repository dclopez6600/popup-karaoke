Karaoke in Gary, Indiana — 18th Street Brewery & Private Events

By PopUp Karaoke · Gary, IN · May 2026 · 5 min read

Gary, Indiana has an extraordinary musical legacy — this is the hometown of the Jackson 5, one of the most influential groups in American pop history. Karaoke in Gary carries that energy. Here's where to find it, and how to bring it to your own event.

Weekly Karaoke at 18th Street Brewery — Every Wednesday

18th Street Brewery in Gary's Miller Beach neighborhood is one of the best spots in all of NW Indiana for a night out — a craft brewery with a cool lakefront-adjacent vibe, a loyal regular crowd, and PopUp Karaoke live every Wednesday night. We bring the full professional setup: PA speakers, LED stage lighting, wireless mics, and 75,000+ songs.

Miller Beach itself is worth the trip — a creative arts community on Lake Michigan's shoreline, with a relaxed character that's unlike any other corner of NW Indiana. The brewery draws a diverse, open crowd: artists, longtime Gary residents, visitors from Chicago and the south suburbs, and regulars who sing the same song every week and somehow make it better each time. Walk-ins are welcome every Wednesday — no reservation, no cover, just show up and put your name in.

The Gary Karaoke Crowd: What to Expect

Gary crowds take the mic seriously in the best possible way. This is a city with music in its DNA — people here know how to listen, how to cheer, and how to bring real energy to a performance. A good singer in Gary gets a real reaction. A brave first-timer gets encouraged. The atmosphere at 18th Street on a Wednesday night is consistently one of our favorite places to host.

The song selection at Gary events tends toward R&B, soul, classic hip-hop, and gospel-influenced pop — but we see plenty of classic rock and country requests too, and the crowd is always enthusiastic regardless of what comes up next. Our 75,000-song library means no genre is underrepresented.

Private Mobile Karaoke Events in Gary

Beyond the Wednesday night residency, we handle private events throughout Gary — birthdays, family reunions, church anniversaries, corporate outings, and community celebrations. Gary has a number of solid event venues, banquet halls, and community spaces, and we've worked in many of them. We bring the same full setup we'd bring anywhere in Lake County — sound, lighting, host, and library — and we size the PA to the room.

Healthcare and education employers in the Gary area have used us for employee appreciation events and team-building nights. We're COI-ready for any corporate or institutional booking that requires it.

Gary's Karaoke Scene Beyond the Brewery

If you're looking for karaoke in Gary beyond our Wednesday residency, the bar and event scene has options across the city — from established taverns in the Tolleston neighborhood to bars and grills along Broadway and 5th Avenue. The city's nightlife is more active than its national reputation suggests, especially in Miller Beach and the revitalizing downtown corridor. We can bring mobile karaoke to any of these venues for a private or semi-private event — contact us and we'll work out the logistics.
