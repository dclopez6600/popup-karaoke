Karaoke in Highland, Indiana — Mobile Entertainment & Private Events

By PopUp Karaoke · Highland, IN · June 2026 · 4 min read

Highland is one of NW Indiana's most stable, well-established suburbs — a community that knows what it likes and shows up for good entertainment. Whether you're a bar owner looking to add a karaoke night or hosting a private event at home, PopUp Karaoke brings the full professional experience to Highland.

The Highland Entertainment Market

Highland borders Hammond to the north, Munster to the west, and Griffith to the south — it sits at the center of the densest part of Lake County. That means any karaoke night here draws from a wider pool than just Highland residents: people from neighboring towns come to Highland bars regularly because the options are varied and the commute is short.

Highland's commercial strip on Indianapolis Boulevard has a solid mix of bars and restaurants that do consistent business. If you're a venue considering a karaoke night, the built-in foot traffic already exists — we're just here to give people a reason to stay.

Highland Private Event Karaoke

Highland is a very social community — block parties, neighborhood cookouts, and backyard events are part of the culture here. We do a lot of private residential karaoke in Highland: the setups are informal, the crowds are friendly, and the events typically run longer than planned because nobody wants to stop.

We also handle formal Highland events: banquet halls, retirement communities, school fundraisers, and corporate gatherings. Our all-ages setup is completely configurable — the same system that runs a college bar night can run a 60th birthday party at a country club.
