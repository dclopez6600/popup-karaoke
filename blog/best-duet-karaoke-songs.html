47 Best Duet Karaoke Songs for Couples, Friends & Coworkers

By Daniel Lopez · PopUp Karaoke · NW Indiana & Chicagoland · October 2026 · 8 min read

A great duet is the best moment of any karaoke night. Two people, one song, and a crowd cheering them on. Whether you're singing with your partner, your best friend, or a coworker you just met, here are the duets that work every time, sorted so you can find the right one for your voices and your room.

How to Pick the Right Duet

  • Match the range. Many classic duets are written for one lower and one higher voice, but any two people can sing any duet. The host can change the key if a song sits too high or too low.
  • Pick songs you both know. A familiar duet sung with confidence beats an impressive one you're reading for the first time.
  • Decide who sings what before you start. Most duets trade verses and come together on the chorus. Agree on the split so you're not both jumping in on the same line.
  • Think about the room. A romantic ballad is perfect at a wedding. At a work party, pick something fun instead.

Classic Duets

The timeless picks. Nearly every guest knows these.

  1. "Don't Go Breaking My Heart" — Elton John & Kiki Dee · The feel-good duet. Back-and-forth lines make it easy to split.
  2. "Endless Love" — Diana Ross & Lionel Richie · The classic slow duet for couples who can carry a ballad.
  3. "Up Where We Belong" — Joe Cocker & Jennifer Warnes · A big, sweeping chorus that rewards commitment.
  4. "I Got You Babe" — Sonny & Cher · Simple, sweet, and easy for beginners.
  5. "Ain't No Mountain High Enough" — Marvin Gaye & Tammi Terrell · Joyful Motown. Everyone in the room sings the chorus.
  6. "It Takes Two" — Marvin Gaye & Kim Weston · A deeper Motown cut that always surprises the crowd.
  7. "Somethin' Stupid" — Frank Sinatra & Nancy Sinatra · Low-key and charming. Great for nervous singers.
  8. "Leather and Lace" — Stevie Nicks & Don Henley · Soft and harmonious for a quieter moment.
  9. "Stop Draggin' My Heart Around" — Stevie Nicks & Tom Petty · Rock attitude with a lot of personality.
  10. "Separate Lives" — Phil Collins & Marilyn Martin · The dramatic breakup duet. Lean into it.
  11. "Nothing's Gonna Stop Us Now" — Starship · Pure 80s optimism with a huge chorus.
  12. "Don't Give Up" — Peter Gabriel & Kate Bush · Moving and beautiful for strong singers.

Country Duets

Country music has the deepest bench of duets in karaoke.

  1. "Picture" — Kid Rock & Sheryl Crow · A bar-night favorite. Trades verses perfectly.
  2. "Jackson" — Johnny Cash & June Carter · Fast, fun, and full of attitude.
  3. "Golden Ring" — George Jones & Tammy Wynette · Classic country storytelling for two.
  4. "After the Fire Is Gone" — Loretta Lynn & Conway Twitty · Old-school country harmony.
  5. "Louisiana Woman, Mississippi Man" — Loretta Lynn & Conway Twitty · Playful back-and-forth that crowds love.
  6. "Whiskey Lullaby" — Brad Paisley & Alison Krauss · A tearjerker for singers who can go soft.
  7. "Don't You Wanna Stay" — Jason Aldean & Kelly Clarkson · A modern country power ballad.
  8. "Need You Now" — Lady A · The late-night duet everyone recognizes from the first line.

Modern Pop & R&B Duets

For crowds that know the current charts.

  1. "Shallow" — Lady Gaga & Bradley Cooper · The duet of the decade. The big note gets a standing ovation.
  2. "Die with a Smile" — Lady Gaga & Bruno Mars · A recent hit with a timeless feel.
  3. "APT." — ROSÉ & Bruno Mars · Playful and catchy. The crowd chants along.
  4. "Golden" — KPop Demon Hunters · A current favorite that friend groups love to sing together.
  5. "Leave the Door Open" — Silk Sonic · Smooth and soulful. Perfect for showing off a falsetto.
  6. "Señorita" — Shawn Mendes & Camila Cabello · A modern pop duet with an easy chorus.
  7. "Say Something" — A Great Big World & Christina Aguilera · Gentle and emotional.
  8. "Stay" — Rihanna & Mikky Ekko · Slow and powerful. The piano carries you.
  9. "Just Give Me a Reason" — P!nk & Nate Ruess · A conversation set to music, with a great call-and-response.
  10. "Lucky" — Jason Mraz & Colbie Caillat · Sweet, easy, and perfect for first-time duet partners.
  11. "Bring Me to Life" — Evanescence · One singer belts, the other brings the rock vocal. Huge payoff.

Movie & Musical Duets

Big, theatrical, and fun to perform.

  1. "(I've Had) The Time of My Life" — Dirty Dancing · The ultimate party duet. Someone will attempt the lift.
  2. "You're the One That I Want" — Grease · High energy and instantly recognizable.
  3. "A Whole New World" — Aladdin · A Disney classic that every generation knows.
  4. "Love Is an Open Door" — Frozen · Fast, funny, and great for friends.
  5. "Rewrite the Stars" — The Greatest Showman · Big, theatrical, and beautiful.
  6. "Suddenly Seymour" — Little Shop of Horrors · A musical theater gem with a huge finish.

Fun Duets for Friends & Coworkers

Not every duet has to be romantic. These are made for laughing.

  1. "Under Pressure" — Queen & David Bowie · Two rock legends' parts. Everyone does the bass line.
  2. "Don't You Want Me" — The Human League · An 80s synth-pop duet with a story.
  3. "Love Shack" — The B-52's · A party song that turns into a group number.
  4. "Barbie Girl" — Aqua · Ridiculous in the best way. Commit to the voices.
  5. "Paradise by the Dashboard Light" — Meat Loaf · Eight minutes of drama. Save it for a crowd that's all in.
  6. "Telephone" — Lady Gaga & Beyoncé · Two divas, one stage.

Latin Duets

From our extensive Spanish-language catalog.

  1. "Despacito" — Luis Fonsi & Daddy Yankee · The global hit everyone can sing along to.
  2. "La bicicleta" — Carlos Vives & Shakira · Breezy, upbeat, and fun.
  3. "Bailando" — Enrique Iglesias · High energy with a chorus the room joins in.
  4. "Hips Don't Lie" — Shakira & Wyclef Jean · Dance floor guaranteed.

Tips for a Great Duet Performance

  • Face each other, then the crowd. Singing to your partner on the verses and turning to the audience for the chorus looks natural and confident.
  • Don't fight over the mic. Every PopUp Karaoke setup includes two wireless microphones, so each singer has their own.
  • Lean into the character. Duets like "Jackson" or "Paradise by the Dashboard Light" are half acting. The more you commit, the more the room loves it.
  • Bring the crowd in. On the big chorus, point the mic at the audience. They'll finish it for you.
