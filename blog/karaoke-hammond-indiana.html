Karaoke in Hammond, Indiana — Live Nights & Private Events

By PopUp Karaoke · Hammond, IN · May 2026 · 5 min read

Hammond is one of NW Indiana's most active karaoke markets — a border city with Chicago energy, tight-knit neighborhoods, and a bar scene that stays busy year-round. Here's everything you need to know about finding karaoke in Hammond and how to book your own private night.

Live Karaoke at Flights Taproom — Every 1st & 3rd Tuesday

PopUp Karaoke hosts a regular karaoke residency at Flights Taproom & Whiskey Lounge (839 169th St, Hammond, IN) on the 1st and 3rd Tuesday of every month from 8–11 PM. No cover, no reservation needed — just show up and put your name in the queue. Flights has a great tap selection, a comfortable layout, and regulars who know how to hype up a first-time singer.

It's one of the most consistent open-karaoke nights in Lake County. We bring the full setup — PA speakers, wireless mics, LED lighting, and the complete 75,000-song library. It's not a backing-track YouTube setup; it's the same professional-grade system we bring to weddings and corporate events, just in a bar setting.

What Hammond Karaoke Crowds Are Like

Hammond crowds are a great mix — longtime Hessville and Robertsdale residents who've been going to the same bars for decades, younger people from the south suburbs of Chicago who cross the state line for the vibe, and everybody in between. They're not shy. People in Hammond get up to sing, they cheer for each other, and they request songs that span every decade and genre: Bon Jovi and Cardi B in the same queue, and nobody bats an eye.

The Hammond bar scene is active enough that if you've never tried karaoke before, it's a low-pressure place to start — the crowds are encouraging, not critical. And if you're a confident singer, you'll get the energy back from the room that makes performing actually fun.

Book Private Karaoke in Hammond for Your Event

Beyond the regular bar night at Flights, we handle private karaoke events throughout Hammond — birthdays, wedding receptions, rehearsal dinners, corporate parties, school events, and community gatherings. We bring everything: sound, lighting, host, mics, and the full library. You pick the venue and the guest list; we handle the entertainment.

We know Hammond venues well — halls on the south side, restaurants near the Illinois border, event spaces in north Hammond near Calumet City. If you're not sure where to host, we can point you toward spaces we've worked in before that fit your group size and budget.

Why Choose PopUp Karaoke for Hammond Events

A few things that matter in Hammond specifically:

  • Local and established. We've been the house karaoke at Flights for years — this is home turf, not an out-of-town company charging travel fees.
  • Veteran-owned. Hammond has deep military roots and a strong veteran community. We're proud to be part of it.
  • No Illinois upcharge. Half of Hammond essentially touches Calumet City. We serve both sides of State Line Road at the same price.
  • 75,000+ songs. The Hammond crowd requests everything — Latin, hip-hop, country, classic rock, 80s cheese. We have it all.
