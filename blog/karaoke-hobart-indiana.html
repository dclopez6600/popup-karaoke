Hobart, Indiana

Karaoke in Hobart, Indiana — Mobile Entertainment & Private Events

By PopUp Karaoke · Hobart, IN · June 2026 · 5 min read

Hobart is home to our Friday night residency at El Capitan Bar & Grill — one of the best regular karaoke nights in Lake County. Plus, we handle private bookings throughout the Hobart area for every type of event. Here's everything you need to know about karaoke in Hobart.

Live Karaoke at El Capitan — Every Friday Night

PopUp Karaoke hosts a weekly karaoke residency at El Capitan Bar & Grill in Hobart every Friday night. El Capitan is one of those neighborhood bars that does everything right — good food, full bar, a comfortable room, and a staff that genuinely wants people to have a good time. The karaoke nights reflect that energy.

Friday nights at El Capitan are a mix of regulars who've been coming for months and new faces who found us online or heard about it from a friend. No cover, no reservation needed. Just show up, put your name in the queue, and get ready to sing.

The song library at El Cap runs the full 75,000 songs — everything from current chart hits to deep cuts from the 70s. The crowd there has range: one week you'll hear Whitney Houston followed by Pantera followed by Dolly Parton, and the room will be into all of it.

Hobart's Karaoke Community

Hobart has a genuinely strong local identity. It's not a suburb of something else — it's its own town with its own history, its own crowd, and its own bar culture. That sense of place shows up in karaoke: people in Hobart are there for the night, not just killing time. They cheer loud, they sing hard, and they stick around.

Hobart also sits right on the edge of Lake County and Porter County, drawing from Merrillville to the west and Portage and Valparaiso to the east. Friday nights at El Capitan pull from a wider area than just the immediate neighborhood.

Private Karaoke Events in Hobart

Beyond the El Capitan residency, we handle private karaoke events throughout Hobart. Birthday parties, wedding afterparties, graduation celebrations, corporate events — Hobart has a strong event-hosting culture and we're set up to serve it well.

We're familiar with the Hobart area event spaces — banquet halls, park shelters, and restaurant private dining rooms. If you're not sure where to host, we can help you think through options that fit your guest count and budget.

Book Karaoke in Hobart

For the El Capitan open karaoke night, just show up on Friday. For private bookings, call or text 219.758.1313 or fill out the booking form. We're local, we know Hobart well, and there are no travel fees for Lake County events.
