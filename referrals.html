How it works

Three Steps,
No Codes Required

  1. Tell a friend about PopUp Karaoke

    Share this page, send them your experience, or just brag about how much your party ruled. Point them to popupkaraoke.net or have them call 219.758.1313.

  2. They mention your name when booking

    When your friend reaches out to book, they just mention your name as the person who referred them. That's it — no code to remember, no form to fill out.

  3. You both get $25 off

    Once their event is confirmed, you'll hear from us with your $25 credit toward your next booking. Your friend's $25 discount is applied automatically to their event.

The fine print

Program Details

  • The referral discount applies when your friend books and pays a deposit for a new PopUp Karaoke event.
  • There's no limit on how many friends you can refer — each successful referral earns you another $25 credit.
  • Your $25 credit is applied toward your next event booking. Credits don't expire and don't have to be used all at once.
  • Your friend's $25 discount is applied to their first PopUp Karaoke event total.
  • Referrals must come from a previous paying customer. Current open bookings don't count until the event is complete.
  • The referral only counts if your friend mentions your name when booking — so make sure they know to say it.
  • Discounts cannot be combined with other promotional offers. One referral discount per booking.
  • PopUp Karaoke reserves the right to modify or end this program with reasonable notice.
