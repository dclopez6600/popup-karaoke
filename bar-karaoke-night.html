Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your first karaoke night.

Where can I find karaoke near me in NW Indiana?
PopUp Karaoke runs weekly shows at three bars: El Capitán in Hobart (Fridays), 18th Street Brewery in Gary (Wednesdays), and Flights Taproom in Hammond (Tuesdays). All shows are free to attend — see the schedule above.
Is there a cover charge?
No cover charge, ever. Our bar karaoke nights are completely free to attend. Just show up, grab a drink, and put your name in for a song.
How do I sign up to sing?
Walk up to the PopUp Karaoke host, write your name and song on a slip, and we'll call you up when it's your turn. We rotate singers throughout the night so everyone gets a chance.
What songs do you have?
75,000+ songs — pop, country, hip-hop, rock, R&B, oldies, Spanish, K-pop and more. Browse the full list at popupkaraoke.net/catalog.
Can I book a private karaoke event at a bar?
Absolutely. We do venue buyouts, bachelorette parties, birthday nights, corporate events, and more. Fill out our booking form or email us at booking@popupkaraoke.net.
Do you travel to Chicago?
Yes — we serve all of Chicagoland and NW Indiana for private events. Bar residency nights are currently in Indiana only, but we're expanding. Follow us to stay updated.
