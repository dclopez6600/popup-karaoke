Finding Us

Looking for Karaoke Near Me?

If you're searching for "karaoke near me" in NW Indiana or the south suburbs of Chicago, there's a good chance we're the answer. PopUp Karaoke is a veteran-owned mobile karaoke service based in the region — we bring the full professional setup to your location, wherever you are in our service area.

Unlike a karaoke bar, we come to you. That means your house, your venue, your backyard, your company's event space — you control the guest list, the playlist vibe, and the drinks. We handle the sound, the songs, the mics, the lighting, and the hosting.
