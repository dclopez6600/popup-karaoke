Daniel Lopez served in the U.S. Army. He doesn't just understand what it means to serve — he lived it. Veteran Event FAQ

Are you really veteran-owned? +

Yes. PopUp Karaoke was founded by Daniel Lopez, a U.S. Army veteran. Daniel brings the discipline and dedication from his military service into every event he runs. You're not hiring a company that slapped a flag on their logo — you're hiring a fellow veteran.

Do you have patriotic and military songs? +

Yes. Our 75,000+ song library includes an extensive catalog of patriotic anthems, country classics, and military-era hits. We work with you before the event to set up the right song selection — so the night sounds exactly right for the crowd and the occasion.

Can you come to a VFW or American Legion post? +

Yes. We've worked at VFW and American Legion halls throughout NW Indiana. We're familiar with the venue types and the culture. We set up, run the show professionally, and tear down without disrupting post operations.

Do you offer any veteran discounts? +

We support our veteran community. Contact us directly to discuss your event and we'll take care of you. We believe in investing in the people who invested in this country — reach out and let's talk.

How far do you travel for military events? +

We serve all of NW Indiana and the greater Chicagoland area. Travel fees may apply for events outside our standard service zone. Contact us with your venue location and we'll give you a direct answer.
