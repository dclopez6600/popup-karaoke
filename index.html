Our Story

More Than Just Karaoke

Established in 2020, PopUp Karaoke is a proudly veteran-owned mobile karaoke and event entertainment service for Northwest Indiana and Chicagoland. What started as a hobby grew into the region's premier full-service karaoke experience.

We specialize in high-energy fun for every occasion — corporate parties, team-building, weddings, birthdays, bars and more. Don't just book a booth — book an unforgettable show!

Our Promise

Karaoke for Everyone

The mic is for everyone — no exceptions.

PopUp Karaoke is a welcoming space where every person — regardless of age, background, ability, or how well they can carry a tune — deserves to feel comfortable on that stage. We expect everyone at our events to treat each other with respect: singers, audience, and staff alike. Harassment, discrimination, or any behavior that makes someone feel unwelcome has no place here.

If something feels off at one of our shows, tell us directly — flag our host, text 219.758.1313, or send us a message. We'll handle it quickly and discreetly.

If it's not fun for everyone, it's not fun. 🎤
