About the El Capitán Residency

El Capitán on Main Street in Hobart is one of NW Indiana's most beloved spots — amazing seafood, killer Mexican food, strong drinks, and a warm, vibrant atmosphere. Every single Friday, PopUp Karaoke sets up shop and turns it into one of the best karaoke nights in the region.

Our setup fills the room with concert-quality sound and stage lighting, making every singer sound and look great. With 75,000+ songs spanning every genre — including an extensive Spanish-language catalog — there's something for everyone. El Capitán's diverse crowd brings enormous energy to these nights, especially when the late-night sets hit their stride around 10 PM.

The 4-hour block (8pm to midnight) means more songs, longer queues, and a night that genuinely feels like a show. Our host keeps the pacing tight, makes every singer feel like a star, and reads the room to keep energy high from the first song to the last call.

What to Expect on a Karaoke Friday

  • Eat first — El Capitán's food is outstanding; dinner + karaoke is the move
  • Arrive by 8pm to claim your spot in the queue early
  • Late night energy — the crowd builds all night, peaking around 10–11 PM
  • Spanish-language catalog — we carry reggaeton, cumbia, banda, boleros, and Latin pop
  • Stay till close — late-night karaoke has a different kind of magic

Frequently Asked Questions

When is karaoke at El Capitán?
Every Friday, 8:00 PM to midnight. Check our events calendar for the full list of upcoming dates.
Where is El Capitán?
327 Main St, Hobart, IN 46342. Get directions →
Is there a cover charge?
No — it's completely free to attend. Just walk in, grab a seat, order some food and drinks, and put in a song request.
Do you have Spanish-language songs?
Absolutely — our library includes a huge Spanish-language catalog: reggaeton, cumbia, banda, boleros, mariachi classics, and Latin pop. Great for bilingual nights at El Capitán.
How late does karaoke go?
Midnight — making it one of the only late-night karaoke events in the area. Perfect for a Friday night out that doesn't end at 10.
