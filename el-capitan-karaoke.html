What to Expect at El Capitán Karaoke Night

PopUp Karaoke hosts karaoke at El Capitán Seafood & Mexican Grill on Main Street in Hobart every Friday. It's a real show, not a bar machine in the corner: a professional host runs the night, the sound system is the same concert-quality setup we bring to weddings and corporate events, and the lyrics are on a big screen so everyone can follow along.

First-timers are welcome. The crowd cheers for everyone, the host keeps the queue moving, and there's no pressure to sing. Plenty of people come just to watch, grab dinner or a drink, and sing along from their seats.

How to Sign Up

  • From your phone: tap Join the Queue and pick your song.
  • In person: sign up with the host when you arrive.
  • Plan ahead: search our 90,000+ songs before you go, or see what's new this month.

Age rules, food, and drinks are set by El Capitán. Please check with the venue before bringing guests under 21.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is karaoke at El Capitán?

PopUp Karaoke hosts karaoke at El Capitán every Friday from 8:00 PM – 12:00 AM. Check the upcoming dates on this page or the events calendar at popupkaraoke.net for this month's schedule.

Is karaoke at El Capitán free?

Yes. There's no cover for karaoke night. Just show up, sign up to sing, and enjoy the show.

How do I sign up to sing?

Join the queue from your phone using the Queue link on popupkaraoke.net, or sign up with the host when you arrive. You can also plan your songs ahead with our song search.

What songs can I sing?

Our library has 90,000+ songs, including current hits, classic rock, country, R&B, hip-hop, Latin, and musicals. Search the full catalog at popupkaraoke.net/catalog.html.

Is it all ages?

Age rules are set by El Capitán. Please check with the venue before bringing guests under 21.

Can I book PopUp Karaoke for my own party?

Yes. We bring the same host, sound, lights, and song library to birthdays, weddings, corporate events, and more across NW Indiana and Chicagoland. Book at popupkaraoke.net or call 219.758.1313.
