Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 18, 2026  ·  PopUp Karaoke, LLC

PopUp Karaoke ("we", "us", or "our") operates the PopUp Karaoke mobile application and website (together, the "Service"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service.

Information We Collect

The App does not require you to create an account to browse events, the song catalog, or reviews. We may collect anonymous usage analytics (e.g., which screens are most visited) to improve the App experience. We do not sell or share your data with third parties for advertising purposes.

Event & Song Data

Event listings, the song catalog, and reviews displayed in the App are pulled from our servers in real time. This data is read-only from the user's perspective and does not require any personal information.

Push Notifications

If you opt in to push notifications, your device token is stored securely to deliver event reminders and live event alerts. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

Song Requests

When you submit a song request through the App or website, we collect your name and song selection solely to fulfill your request at a live event. This information is not stored beyond the duration of the event.

Contact & Booking Forms

When you submit a quote request or contact form on our website, we collect your name, email address, phone number, and event details. This information is used only to respond to your inquiry and plan your event.

Third-Party Services

The App is built on Expo and React Native. Standard device information (OS version, device model) may be collected by these platforms for crash reporting and performance monitoring. Please review Expo's privacy policy at expo.dev/privacy. Our website uses Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager to understand site traffic anonymously.

Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information we hold about you by contacting us at the email below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the effective date above.
